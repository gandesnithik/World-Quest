📖 How to Play

Each world lasts 15 minutes: three days of five minutes each.

Gather the materials listed at the top left and carry them to the golden beam.

The faster you finish, the more stars you earn.

Five worlds, each harder than the last. In a room, everyone's deliveries count together.

Keyboard

WASD Move · Drag Look · Shift Run · Space Jump

E Pick up or use · F Energy blast · 1 Eat · 2 Drink · I Bag · Enter Chat

Touch screen

Left thumb: move. Pull to the edge to run. Right thumb: look around.

USE picks things up and hands in supplies. JUMP, BLAST and RUN sit beside it. Tap Eat and Drink at the bottom left.

Hunger and thirst fall over time, so eat and drink when you can.

At night, wisps hunt you. Stay by the campfire, or drive them off with an energy blast.

Sleeping at the fire skips to morning but costs two minutes.

Staying up all night wears your health down.

Chests hold supplies.

Stalkers and brutes can be stunned with a blast. Run for the fire.

Thieves grab items from your bag. Hit them with a blast to win it back.

Every world shows a hint. It is worth reading.

On Frostpeak, the cold only bites after dark.

Create a room and share the code with your friends.

The host picks the world.

The chat window can be dragged anywhere.

Stand next to a fallen friend and press Use to bring them back.
